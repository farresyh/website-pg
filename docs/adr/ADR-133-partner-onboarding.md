# ADR-133: Partner onboarding — a two-stage public application with KYC for resellers, and a computed readiness checklist in the portal for every partner

> **Standing (2026-10-31):** Accepted (design). Closes the design of PRD §16 items 13 (self-serve reseller signup) and 146 (onboarding). Extends [ADR-072](./ADR-072-reseller-system-split.md) (accounts are still admin-provisioned in the end), [ADR-131](./ADR-131-chip-activation-conditions.md) condition 4 (documents collected from each reseller) and [ADR-132](./ADR-132-affiliate-monthly-auto-payout.md) decision 12 (the bank-details nudge it deferred). Touches no price, ledger or voucher write; two decisions touch the wallet top-up seam and the markup cap (20, 22) and are traced before they are built.

**Status:** Accepted (design), 2026-10-31. Grilled with the founder in four rounds, then stress-tested against the code (seven findings, folded into the decisions below). Nothing is built.

## Context

There is no onboarding anywhere: no checklist, first-run screen, readiness flag or pending state (traced 2026-10-31, backend, portal, admin, storefront). A new partner can get stuck silently:

- An affiliate's dashboard shows "Live" when the domain is Active, while every checkout is refused (`payments_approved_at` null, [ADR-131](./ADR-131-chip-activation-conditions.md)). Nothing tells them, and the portal's "we will let you know" has no notification behind it.
- Missing bank details make the monthly payout skip the affiliate every month with no notice (`skip = no_bank`, `AffiliateWithdrawalService.php:79`). The admin cannot enter bank details for them.
- A reseller with no tier gets `NO_TIER_ASSIGNED` on every API and bot order, but Add Balance still works, so they can fund an account that cannot order. Creating a reseller makes no portal login; that is a separate admin step.
- The admin cannot see who is stuck: neither list has a readiness column.

A reseller account can only be opened by the admin today (ADR-072/076, PRD item 13 "not designed"). [ADR-131](./ADR-131-chip-activation-conditions.md) condition 4 says CHIP expects the documents collected from each reseller; the onboarding procedure and the documents live outside the repo (decision 24 there). The founder wants applicants to apply on a public page and to be screened **before** any identity document is collected, so documents are never held for people who will not be accepted.

Facts the design rests on (read from code, 2026-10-31): the existing sandbox ([ADR-018](./ADR-018-sandbox-test-orders.md)) is admin-only and the reseller paths never mention `is_test`, so a partner has no sandbox; the portal's `proxy.ts` sends every session-less path except `/login`, `/set-password` and `/impersonate` to `/login`; `affiliate_users.email` is unique and a closed account only deactivates its users; the Spatie backup is a SQL dump only (`include => []`), so files are never in it; `TurnstileVerifier` exists and fails closed; wallet top-up has one seam, `ResellerWalletTopupService::initiate()`, used by both the portal and `.topupbaki`.

## Decision

### A. Public application and KYC (resellers only)

1. **Resellers only.** An affiliate stays admin-invited: its branded domain needs CHIP's written approval per domain anyway (ADR-131 decision 19–22), so an open form saves nothing. Affiliates still get Part B.
2. **Two stages, one table.** `partner_applications` holds one row per applicant: a `status` that spans both stages (`email_pending`, `new`, `invited`, `documents_submitted`, `approved`, `declined`), the stage-1 answers, a hashed token, `token_expires_at`, `notice_version`, a consent timestamp per stage, and a nullable unique `reseller_id`. `partner_application_documents` holds one row per file. No separate "enquiry" table.
3. **Stage 1 asks no document.** Owner name, company, phone, email; reseller experience (how long, which platforms); estimated monthly sales as a range, shown to the admin as "self-declared, not verified"; main games; how they plan to order (API, WhatsApp bot, not sure); how they heard of us; one consent box. The email is verified by a link before the application counts.
4. **Host and app.** `onboarding.pekangame.space` is served by the existing `reseller/` app, outside `(portal)` (public routes `/apply` and `/onboard/[token]`), not a fifth app. `proxy.ts` splits by host: the onboarding host serves only those routes, the portal host serves neither. `noindex`. The backend takes the submissions on its own unauthenticated route group with throttles and `TurnstileVerifier`. The widget's hostnames must include the onboarding host (founder action, same as ADR-123).
5. **Email identity.** An email that belongs to any portal user, active or closed, is refused at stage 1 ("contact us"); one active application per email. Approval re-checks inside its transaction; the unique constraint is the last guard.
6. **Admin screen.** A list and a detail page that reads like a form. Actions: **Send stage-2 link**, **Decline** (one short polite email, no reasons, once), **Invite directly** (creates an application already at stage 2, for someone the founder knows), **Regenerate link**, **Delete applicant data** (a data-subject request).
7. **Stage-2 link.** A long random token, stored as a hash, single use, 14 days, sent only to the verified stage-1 email. Progress can be saved and resumed until submitted, then it locks. Regenerating revokes the old token. No login.
8. **Documents** follow the founder's CHIP list: the owner's identity, business details, SSM, proof of business address, one or two months of a bank statement (redacting transaction lines is allowed, because they hold third parties' data), and the tax identification number. An individual without SSM is not accepted yet. PDF, JPG or PNG, a size cap, the real file type checked (not the extension). Storage is **private**, never a public URL; the admin views a file through a link valid for 5 minutes, and every view is logged (who, when). No OCR or automatic verification; the founder reads them.
9. **Retention.** A declined application's rows and files are deleted after 90 days by one scheduled, idempotent command. An approved applicant's documents stay while the account is open and for a period after closure (**owed**, decision 25). The notice says plainly that a deleted row can remain in older database dumps until they expire (the dump is SQL only; files are never in it).
10. **PDPA (Malaysia).** Two notices, one per stage, each **bilingual (Malay and English)** because section 7 requires both; each with its own consent box. `notice_version` and the consent time are stored on the application. The notices are versioned text in code, **written with a lawyer** (draft kept outside the repo until reviewed). Rights to access, correct and delete go through a named contact; the admin's delete action serves them. Cross-border transfers (private storage region, Plunk email, Cloudflare Turnstile) are recorded in this ADR's consequence list. The breach procedure (72-hour notice to the Commissioner, as reported by secondary sources, to be checked against the official guideline) is a document outside the repo.
11. **Approval is one service.** `PartnerApplicationService` owns every status transition under a row lock; approving twice cannot make a second reseller (`reseller_id` unique). "Approve" must pick a **tier** and calls `ResellerProvisioningService` (decision 21). The invite email is queued **after commit**, not inside the transaction (unlike the affiliate create, where a Plunk failure rolls everything back); "Resend invite" already exists.
12. **Three ways to get a reseller.** (a) Public apply; (b) the admin's **Invite directly**; (c) the admin's **Create internal account**, kept for test or internal accounts (FixFast for CHIP): super_admin with MFA step-up, a required reason in the audit log, and the list shows "No KYC on file". Existing resellers stay as they are (no migration; their documents were collected outside the system, the list says "KYC outside the system", not an error).

### B. Readiness and first-run in the portal

13. **One read-only `PartnerReadinessService`** returns the steps and each step's state (`done`, `todo`, `waiting_on_us`, `optional`) for an affiliate or a reseller, with `forMany()` for the admin lists (eager-loads, no N+1; no cache, because stale readiness is worse; `ponytail:` a few hundred partners). It **never recomputes a gate that already exists**: it calls the same predicates the real code uses. Bank presence becomes one `Affiliate::hasBankDetails()`, which the two existing copies (`Affiliate/WithdrawalController.php:61`, `AffiliateWithdrawalService.php:79`) and the service all call; domain Active and payments approval use the existing domain and `StorefrontBrand` checks; "first order" uses the same paid, `is_test = false` scope the reports use. One invariant test: "ready to sell" is true exactly when a checkout on that store can pay.
14. **Affiliate steps:** password (already done on arrival, so the list starts at 1 of N), bank details, connect domain, DNS verified (with the deadline: a domain pending 14 days is torn down), payments approval (shown as "waiting on us", never promising a date: CHIP's written approval comes first), branding (logo and a theme other than PekanGame's default), review markup, first order. Required to sell: password, domain, DNS, approval. An owned brand skips bank and markup. **Reseller steps:** password, add Purchase Balance, choose how to order (an active API key **or** a linked WhatsApp group, defaulting to the stage-1 answer), first order, webhook (optional). Tier is not a step: approval always sets it. For the bot, the portal shows the instructions and the link state, which it never mentioned.
15. **Banners and the checklist.** The checklist can be collapsed (browser `localStorage`, no column) and collapses itself when every required step is done. A banner that cannot be dismissed appears only for what costs money or blocks selling: an affiliate with no bank details; a domain that is Active but not approved ("Your store is online, payments are not enabled yet"); an older reseller with no tier. The dashboard's "Live" badge splits into **Online** and **Ready to sell**.
16. **First order and empty states.** There is no partner sandbox (see "Not decided here"), so a reseller's first order is real money. A "Your first order" card says: use a small package, check the player ID first, a failed order returns to the Purchase Balance. Empty Orders shows one button (an affiliate: copy the store link, or "connect your domain first"; a reseller: choose how to order); empty Balance shows Add Balance.
17. **Notifications.** An email to the affiliate when the admin approves payments (fulfils the portal's existing promise); an item in the admin's "Needs attention" list when a domain is Active and not approved. Approval stays a manual step after CHIP's written approval (PRD §16 item 141).
18. **Admin lists** get a readiness column from the same service ("Ready" or "Needs: bank, approval").
19. **Language.** The portal, the public forms and emails to applicants are English; the set-password page (Malay today) becomes English. The two privacy notices stay bilingual by law (decision 10).

### C. Fixes folded in (each its own PR)

20. **A reseller with no tier cannot add balance.** The guard sits in `ResellerWalletTopupService::initiate()`, the one seam the portal and `.topupbaki` share, and **never** in `completeTopup()`: a top-up already paid must still credit. The admin's manual credit stays allowed. A test starts a top-up, removes the tier, and proves it still completes.
21. **One `ResellerProvisioningService`** (reseller + ledger account + portal user + invite, tier required) used by decision 11 and by both admin create paths, so the admin create makes the login too and no second copy exists.
22. **`max_markup_pct` null** means "no ceiling" for the admin form and the pricing code, but the portal's markup edit casts it to 0 and answers "at most 0%". Fixed as its own PR after a trace of every writer and reader of the column.
23. **Set-password language** (decision 19).

### Not decided here
- **A partner sandbox** (a test-type API key: orders flagged `is_test`, no Purchase Balance debit, `FakeSupplierAdapter` outcomes, webhook still sent) is its own money-critical ADR after this one: it must be proved not to touch the wallet, ledger, reports, receipts, rate limits or the bot. The checklist's first-order step changes to "test in the sandbox, then order" when it exists. Refreshing the admin sandbox for wallet and combo orders is a separate backlog line.
- Affiliate public signup, a store preview mode for affiliates, OCR, a "Go live" status flag and daily nudge emails: not planned.

## Rationale

- **Documents only after screening** removes the harm of holding IC and bank statements for people the founder will not accept, and makes stage 1 cheap to read.
- **A separate application table** leaves every money path untouched: no code that places an order, tops up or logs in learns a "pending" reseller.
- **One readiness service reading real gates** removes the drift that two copies of the bank check already show; a third copy would eventually disagree with the payout.
- **Computed, not flagged:** no new status to keep in sync with the admin's manual approval step.
- **The guard at `initiate()`** covers the portal and the bot with one change, and leaving `completeTopup()` alone keeps paid money safe.

## Consequence to track

- **Owed by the founder:** the CHIP document list in final form; a lawyer's review of both notices; the retention period after closure (ask the accountant); the Turnstile widget hostname; confirm the private storage bucket exists on the Forge box and its region (decision 8 and 10; until confirmed, the local private disk is the fallback and its single copy is a known risk); whether CHIP receives copies of the documents (the notice lists it only if so).
- **Build order:** PR 1 `Affiliate::hasBankDetails()`, `ResellerProvisioningService`, `PartnerReadinessService` with the invariant tests, then the checklist, banners, empty states and emails; PR 2 the top-up guard (decision 20, wallet trace first) and the admin create fix; PR 3 the public forms, the admin screen and the retention command; the `max_markup_pct` fix is its own PR.
- **Code trace before PR 2:** every caller of `ResellerWalletTopupService::initiate()` and of `completeTopup()`; before the markup PR, every writer and reader of `max_markup_pct`.
- Item 13 is closed into this ADR. Open PRD lines: the sandbox ADR and the admin sandbox refresh (group D).
- **Facts checked from secondary sources only** (law-firm summaries, 2026-10-31): the 2025 PDPA amendment dates, the 72-hour and 7-day breach windows, the DPO thresholds. Re-check them against the Commissioner's text; no guidance on collecting NRIC numbers was found.
